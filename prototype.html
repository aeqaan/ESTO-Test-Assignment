Reminder with a way out

Anna's €57.40 hire purchase payment is due on 15 December. Three days before, a free reminder email arrives. One tap to pay — or, if money is short this month, one tap to see all her options in one place, before she's late.

Today vs. here

Today a payment holiday and a lower monthly payment sit in different parts of the portal, and a new date means emailing the debt team. Here they're in one place: one question suggests the best fit, every option stays visible, and the terms show before she chooses.

Try both paths

  • See options → “Next month” → payment holiday
  • Pay €57.40 → choose a bank → paid

Success metric: share of invoices paid on time, A/B test over 2 billing cycles. More in the write-up.

Fees and amounts are illustrative.
